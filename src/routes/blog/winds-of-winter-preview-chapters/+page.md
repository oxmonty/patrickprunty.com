I'm a **huge** [_A Song of Ice and Fire_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Song_of_Ice_and_Fire)
fan. So, naturally, after reading the entire series again for the third time, I
checked whether there was news about the upcoming release of the next book in
the series, _The Winds of Winter_. And of course, fifteen years on from the last
published book, there's nothing to indicate it'll be released any time soon.

But I was surprised and excited to find there's been no less than nine drafted
chapters publicly released by George R.R. Martin via different sources. This
comprises over 100 pages of content that we can sink our teeth into whilst we
await the release.

You can download the preview chapters in different formats here (.mobi for
putting the book on your Kindle).
